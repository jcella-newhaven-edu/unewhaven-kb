# Removing an Email Account from a Desktop Mail Client

Students, faculty, and staff can remove an email account from the following desktop mail clients: Outlook for Windows, Outlook for Mac, Apple Mail.

## Removing an Email Account from Outlook for Windows
1. Select File.
2. Select Account Settings  >  Account Settings.
3. Select the account you want to delete, and then select Remove.
4. Select Yes to confirm.

## Removing an Email Account from Outlook for Mac
Open Outlook.
Navigate to Tools > Accounts.
Select the account you want to remove, and then click the minus sign (–) at the bottom left corner of the Accounts window.
Click Delete.

## Removing an Email Account from Apple Mail
Open Apple Mail.
Navigate to Mail > Accounts…
Select the account you want to remove.
Click the minus sign (–) at the bottom left corner of the Accounts window.
Click OK.
